# Changelog

All notable changes to B45 Labs | Wayfinder are documented here.
This project adheres to [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/) and
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.0.1] - 2026-11-15

> The first release of B45 Labs | Wayfinder for Autodesk Navisworks Manage 2026: fly through the model in first person, group clashes with a preview before anything changes, and put a section box around a clash, together with the B45 Labs tools you already know from Revit.

### Added

- **FlyNav, first-person navigation.** Turn it on from the ribbon and move with W, A, S and D, go up with Q and down with E, hold the right mouse button to look around and click with the left button to select. Shift or Ctrl runs, Alt slows down, and the [ and ] keys scale both speeds. It stays on until you click the button again.
- **FlyNav settings.** A small window, open while you fly, where every change applies at once: invert the mouse axes, look sensitivity, walk and run speed, which key runs, and whether the left click selects while flying.
- **FlyNav: jump between saved viewpoints and focus on the selection.** While FlyNav is on, press 1 to 9 to fly to the first nine saved viewpoints of the file (in the order of the Saved Viewpoints window, folders included), or PageUp and PageDown to go to the previous and the next one. Press F to fly to the selected item and frame it, keeping the way you were looking. The camera glides there and any move or look you make stops it where it is. Ctrl and Alt are left alone, so Navisworks shortcuts still work.
- **Group by element.** Groups the clashes of a test by the single element they hit. Before anything changes you see the groups that would be created, can check or uncheck each one, see the clashes inside it and the two items of the selected clash, and zoom to a group. The list on the left holds every clash test in the file: choose one, several or all and they are planned together.
- **Group by item.** Select an item and group every loose clash that involves it, in as many tests as you choose, with the same preview. Clashes keep their status, assignee and comments: they are moved into the group, not copied.
- **Clash Box.** A section box centered on the clash selected in Clash Detective, or on all the clashes of a group. It wraps the whole element and sits parallel to it, and it can follow each new selection until you turn it off. An element much longer than the limit (a 70 m pipe, a footing around the building) is cut to the part near the clash. Remove Box turns sectioning off.
- **Clash Box Settings.** The margin around the element, the longest element kept whole (30 m by default), Follow the selected clash, Apply and Remove.
- **Reload From.** Replace the file of the selected model with another one you choose in Explorer.
- **Main panel: About Me and My Profile.** The same windows as in the other B45 Labs products. My Profile, your logo and your privacy choice are shared with them: what you fill in or choose in one product shows in all of them, and Erase my data works the same way everywhere.
- **Help panel: Tips & Tricks, Plug-in Info, B45 Labs Docs, YouTube Tutorials and Open log folder.** Tips & Tricks shows short videos, news and the whole B45 Labs suite, updated from www.b45labs.com; the content is signed and checked before it is shown. Docs and YouTube ask before they open your browser.
- **Settings panel: Change Theme, Change Language and Plug-in Settings.** The same three tools as in B45 Labs | Coordination, in their own ribbon panel. Change Theme switches the Navisworks interface between its Light and Dark themes (the same setting as Options Editor > Interface > User Interface > Theme) and keeps it for the next session. Plug-in Settings lets you hide ribbon panels and buttons and choose how the Wayfinder windows look: follow the Navisworks theme (Light or Dark, as in Revit) or always use the B45 Labs graphite theme.
- **Five languages.** English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, French and Russian for the ribbon, tooltips, windows and messages. Change Language shows the language Navisworks runs in; if Wayfinder has it you can follow it, and if it does not (German, Japanese, ...) you choose the Wayfinder language yourself. The choice is shared with the other B45 Labs tools.
- **Change the Navisworks language.** Change Language also lists the languages Navisworks itself ships (German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian and both Chinese). Navisworks only reads its language when it starts, so choosing another one offers to restart it: it closes the usual way (asking about unsaved changes) and opens again in that language with the same file. The choice lasts for that session. Autodesk's Portuguese pack for Navisworks is incomplete (most texts stay in English), but the plug-in itself speaks Portuguese.
- **The B45 Labs look.** Every window, message and progress card follows the B45 Labs design: glass windows with the logo and title on top, rounded cards, pill buttons and the same tables as the Coordination. Windows that stay open beside the model can be moved, minimized and closed with Esc, and the grouping window never blocks Navisworks.
- **Anonymous usage and error data, off with one switch.** Like B45 Labs | Coordination, Wayfinder sends anonymous data unless you turn it off in My Profile > Privacy: an installation ID, the Navisworks and Wayfinder versions and language, your approximate region, one event for each ribbon button you click, and errors of Wayfinder itself. It never sends model or file names, paths, your computer or user name, or anything you select or type. Your profile is sent only if you also turn on "Also sync my profile" (see below). Turning it off stops everything and discards what was waiting. My Profile can export and erase your data, and the Privacy Notice lists every field.
- **"Also sync my profile" does what it says.** Off by default. Turn it on in My Profile > Privacy and the profile you saved (name, e-mail, company, role, how you found us) is sent to B45 Labs under the same installation ID as the other B45 Labs products; save the profile again and the new version is sent. Turn it off, or turn off the usage switch, and nothing more is sent. Erase my data asks the B45 Labs servers to delete it first.
- **Update notices and the remote update check.** At startup Wayfinder quietly checks, in the background, whether a newer version exists, using a signed message from B45 Labs. A newer version shows a small card (at most once a day), a version below the minimum shows a window with Exit Navisworks, Later and Update now, and a version that B45 Labs has withdrawn for safety turns the Wayfinder tools off until you update (Exit Navisworks or Update now, no Later). With no connection, or if the message cannot be verified, nothing happens and Wayfinder keeps working. The links only ever lead to b45labs.com or the Wayfinder download.

### Known limits

- Built and tested for Navisworks Manage 2026. A Navisworks 2025 build is prepared but has not been tested yet.
- The Clash Box placement is still being validated on very long and on rotated elements; use Clash Box Settings to tighten the longest element kept whole if a box looks too large.
- Translations are AI-assisted drafts and have not been reviewed by native speakers yet.
- The Navisworks language chosen in Change Language applies to that session only; a normal shortcut opens it in the language of Windows again.
